# Psychologist practice website

A public website introducing one psychologist and the services offered by their practice.

## Language

**Psychologist**:
Polyxeni Petta, the individual professional represented by this website. She is a psychologist currently undertaking four-year training in systemic psychotherapy. Her systemic psychotherapy training is in progress.
_Avoid_: Team, clinic (unless the practice structure changes).

**Service**:
A form of professional support actually offered by the psychologist: individual sessions, group psychotherapy, couples therapy and adolescent psychotherapy. In-person and online sessions are available; do not assume that every format is available online. Group psychotherapy is included in the service copy supplied by the user. Past experience and training do not by themselves establish an additional current service.

**Office**:
The psychologist's private physical practice location in Piraeus.

**Service area**:
The areas whose residents the practice serves, including Athens and Korinthos; this does not imply an office in each area.

**Professional collaboration**:
Work undertaken with another organisation, including PROSOPA in Korinthos. A collaboration does not establish a second private office.

**Online session**:
A session with the psychologist conducted remotely.

**Website language**:
A language in which the website's information is published: Greek or English. This does not establish which languages are offered for clinical sessions.

**Session language**:
A language in which the psychologist offers clinical sessions. Greek and English are both offered, as confirmed by the user.

**Contact page**:
The page presenting the practice's telephone, email, address, and working hours so a visitor can make contact.
_Avoid_: Booking page, enquiry form.
